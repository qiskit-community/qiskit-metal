RouteAnchors
============

Creates and connects a series of anchors through which the Route passes.

.. image:: ../images/qlibrary/RouteAnchors.png
   :alt: RouteAnchors
   :width: 220px

**Class** ``RouteAnchors`` · **Module** ``qiskit_metal.qlibrary.tlines.anchored_path`` · **Category** tlines

**Inherits** ``QRoute`` → ``QComponent``

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: python

   from qiskit_metal import designs
   from qiskit_metal.qlibrary.tlines.anchored_path import RouteAnchors

   design = designs.DesignPlanar()
   comp = RouteAnchors(design, "routeAnchors1", options=dict())
