Open to Ground
==============

A sample_shapes open to ground termination. Functions as a pin for auto drawing.

.. image:: ../images/qlibrary/OpenToGround.png
   :alt: Open to Ground
   :width: 220px

**Class** ``OpenToGround`` · **Module** ``qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.open_to_ground`` · **Category** terminations

**Inherits** ``QComponent``

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: python

   from qiskit_metal import designs
   from qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.open_to_ground import OpenToGround

   design = designs.DesignPlanar()
   comp = OpenToGround(design, "openToGround1", options=dict())
