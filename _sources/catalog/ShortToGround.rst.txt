Short to Ground
===============

A sample_shapes short to ground termination. Functions as a pin for auto CPW

**Class** ``ShortToGround`` · **Module** ``qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.short_to_ground`` · **Category** terminations

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: python

   from qiskit_metal import designs
   from qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.short_to_ground import ShortToGround

   design = designs.DesignPlanar()
   comp = ShortToGround(design, "shortToGround1", options=dict())
