Launchpad Wirebond
==================

Launch pad to feed/read signals to/from the chip.

.. image:: ../images/qlibrary/LaunchpadWirebond.png

   :width: 220px

**Class** ``LaunchpadWirebond`` · **Module** ``qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.launchpad_wb`` · **Category** terminations

**Inherits** ``QComponent``

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: python

   from qiskit_metal import designs
   from qiskit_metal.qlibrary.terminations.launchpad_wb import LaunchpadWirebond

   design = designs.DesignPlanar()
   comp = LaunchpadWirebond(design, "launchpadWirebond1", options=dict())
