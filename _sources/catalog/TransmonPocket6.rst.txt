Transmon Pocket 6
=================

**Class** ``TransmonPocket6`` · **Module** ``qiskit_metal.qlibrary.qubits.transmon_pocket_6`` · **Category** qubits

Usage
-----

.. code-block:: python

   from qiskit_metal import designs
   from qiskit_metal.qlibrary.qubits.transmon_pocket_6 import TransmonPocket6

   design = designs.DesignPlanar()
   comp = TransmonPocket6(design, "transmonPocket61", options=dict())
